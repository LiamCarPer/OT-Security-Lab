# Claims Register

Every headline claim in this repository maps to an artifact and, where possible,
an automated check. This register exists because a portfolio project's
credibility rests on its claims being verifiable — a claim without evidence is a
liability. Update it whenever a claim changes.

**Statuses:** `Verified` (machine-checked on live lab), `Implemented` (built and
tested), `Partial`, `Planned`.
